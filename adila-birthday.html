Dear Adila,

Happy Birthday, sister 🤍

I never officially announced it, and neither did you — but somehow you became family. The kind of family you choose. And honestly? Best decision life ever made for me.

What I love most? You're kind without making it a performance. Funny without even trying. And rare in a way that most people don't even realise — no jealousy, no negativity, just pure good energy. That's you.

And yes — you were my unofficial pen supplier all through university. Sitting in my classroom now, I still feel that small habit of yours missing. Some things just leave a quiet little mark.

with love, Your unofficial brother/friend/family 🌸
